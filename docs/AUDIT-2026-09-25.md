# Deal Board – usability audit and fix plan (25 Sep 2026)

Status: handed to Claude Code for building at Chris's request (25 Sep 2026). The six decisions below use the recommended answers as defaults; Chris has not confirmed them one by one (see docs/PLAN.md). Written 25 Sep 2026 in the Claude project; this copy has client names removed because this repo is public. Covers v16 on the preview link (the version waiting to go live). Live app (v14) has the same core problems.

## How it was checked
- Every screen captured on Chris's S22 size and Annemarie's iPhone 8 size, dark and light (108 screenshots, sample data).
- Text measured on every screen: sizes, contrast, amount, buttons without words.
- 11 everyday jobs walked through, taps counted.
- Real data checked for counts (16 open tasks, 2 deals, 48 deal steps, 438 directory rows, 41 buyer-search steps).
- A second reviewer who had never seen the app went through the screenshots on its own; its findings matched mine on every main point.
Confidence: high on what is measured below; medium on how a real iPhone 8 behaves (tested at its screen size, not on the phone).

## Verdict
The app does a lot, and most of it works. It is hard to use because there is no single place that says "do this next", the same task shows up in several places with different words, most text is small grey print, and many buttons are pictures without words. Contrast is fine (every text passes the standard test in dark and light), so the fix is size, amount, wording and structure – not colours.

## The main problems (worst first)

1. Too many to-do lists. Tasks live in Home (Focus, Schedule, Needs your OK, Buyer search), People, each deal's Waiting tab, the Directory's "Next up" (41 buyer-search steps, a separate system with "gates"), deal checklists (48 steps) and the board. The same task appears twice on Home (under Today and again under Needs your OK).
2. Today's work is not on the first screen. On the iPhone 8 the first screen shows the greeting, an empty brief and one card. The Today list starts about 2 screens down; "Needs your OK" about 3 screens down. Home is 3–3.5 screens long.
3. The confirm step has jammed. 15 of 16 open tasks are still "Proposed / not confirmed". Because unconfirmed tasks are left out of "to chase", Home can say "0 to chase" while showing overdue tasks.
4. One action, many words. Finishing something is "Done", "Got it" or "Keep it" plus a separate tick; ticks, squares and green tiles all look different. Day counts appear as "2 days", "2d", "4d", "overdue 1 day" for similar things.
5. Buttons without words: 6 in the header and bottom bar on every screen, 4 in the task pop-up, 44 on the People page, 10 on the board. The bottom bar has no labels; the "C" circle is the main menu; the magnifier opens "Directory"; the sparkle is the bot.
6. Small, busy text. On Home, 43% of the text is below 14 px (most common size 13 px); Home uses 14 different text sizes; some text goes down to 9 px. Lines stack 3–4 facts with dots ("Proposed · Next: Chase … · Blocks …, then …"). ALL-CAPS tags ("OUR TASK", "GATE", "BUYER").
7. Colour carries meaning (red vs purple box outlines, edge stripes, calendar dots, "Top"/"High" dots) – exactly what Chris can't read.
8. The New task form is backwards: it asks "What are we waiting for?" even for our own job, then asks for names twice; 8 fields; no due date field at all (dates are guessed from "chase after 3 days").
9. People screen shows every control for every task: 63 buttons and boxes at once; the page is 4.4 iPhone 8 screens long.
10. A deal opens inside the Deals list with 9 tabs in a sideways row (only 3 visible); both deals number their stages differently (1., 2. vs A., B.).
11. Hidden screens and dead ends: People, Archive and Calculator are only in the "C" menu; the + "WhatsApp chat" tile just sends you to People with a message to find the person yourself.
12. Numbers that don't agree: "Deal steps 7%" on Home (all deals) vs "8%" on the deal, with nothing saying why; "On track 80%" next to "0 done this week".

## Taps for everyday jobs (S22)

- Add a task for Annemarie: 5 taps plus an 8-field form.
- Tick a deal step: 4 taps plus scrolling.

## Keep
- The + panel with big word tiles (clearest screen in the app).
- Deal checklists that stamp who and when.
- Form labels written as plain questions.
- The board's chat bubbles with deal tags.
- Bot suggests, people decide. Calculator wording.

## Fix plan (small batches; each goes to the preview first and waits for your OK)

Batch 1 – Easy to read (looks only, no workflow change)
- Minimum text 16 px for task text, 14 px for small print; only 3 text sizes; one font; no ALL-CAPS; slightly softer off-white text.
- A word under every bottom-bar icon (Today, Deals, Contacts, Board) and a word on every action button.
- Meaning in words, not colour: "Overdue", "Due today", "Waiting on them", "Our job".
- Dates written out: "Due Sat 26 Sep", "Asked 4 days ago".
- One word per action: Done (finish), Chased (asked again), Accept / Drop (bot suggestions).

Batch 2 – One list (Home becomes the to-do list)
- Home = one list: Overdue, Today, This week, Later; Chris / Annemarie / Both filter at the top; each task one line; tap opens it.
- Bot suggestions sit in the same list marked "Suggested" with Accept / Drop, plus one "Accept all" button (you still decide; the bot never confirms).
- The next buyer-search step and the next step of each deal also appear in this list, so there is nothing to hunt for elsewhere.
- Brief becomes one short line at the top; Progress rings, week strip and swipe cards move below the list (or go – your choice).
- Header counts fixed so they match the list.

Batch 3 – Simple "New task" with due dates
- First choice: "Our job" or "Waiting on someone". Then What, Who does it (Chris / Annemarie buttons), Due (Today / Tomorrow / pick a date). The rest under "More".
- Needs one small database change (a due date on each task).

Batch 4 – Deals as their own page
- Tap a deal: its own page with its name as the title; 4 tabs (Steps, Numbers, Notes & files, Guide); only the current stage open; the calculator inside Numbers.

Batch 5 – One Contacts screen and a clear menu
- Directory, People and Contacts merged into one Contacts screen with search at the top.
- Bottom bar: Today · Deals · + · Contacts · Board. "More" holds Archive, Calculator, Bot, Settings (bot key moves to Settings).
- The + WhatsApp chat tile asks "whose chat?" and imports it there.

Batch 6 – iPhone 8 finish
- Hide the bottom bar while typing; test on Annemarie's phone with her.

Cost: R0 per month (no new paid services). Time: batches 1–2 about one session each; 3–5 one session each.
