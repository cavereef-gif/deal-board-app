## Proposed builds, in order (small batches, each testable)
All are R0 a month (our own build) unless marked.

1. **Load register (transport).** Per load: date, truck registration, driver, tons loaded and offloaded (from the weighbridge tickets), ticket and POD photo, shortage. Totals feed the invoice and the real margin. Done when: a load is logged in under a minute and the month's tons add up.
2. **Money in and out.** A tax invoice PDF that meets the VAT Act (words "Tax Invoice", our VAT number, their VAT number above R5,000, serial number, date, description, VAT); a list of who owes us what and since when (30/60/90 days) with reminders; what we owe the transporter; payouts to each person in the commission split. Done when: "who owes us money?" is one tap.
3. **Trust check per company (fraud).** A short checklist on every seller, buyer and transporter: company found on CIPC, VAT number checked on SARS's free VAT search, bank letter confirmed by phoning the bank (never the number on the letter), site or stockpile seen, proof of product checked independently, references. A warning when someone asks for money up front. Optional gate: no NCNDA until the basics are ticked.
4. **Payment security and assays (minerals).** LC / SBLC / escrow milestones (issued · advised · confirmed · documents presented · paid) and assay results per lot with the price adjustment for grade and moisture.
5. **Expiry reminders.** Dates on insurance certificates, tax PINs, NCNDA terms, transporter papers (goods-in-transit cover, RTMS if the mine asks for it) and site renewals – a reminder before each runs out.
6. **Pipeline value and reports.** Expected commission by month from open deals; profit per client and per route; a tax-year summary (March–February) for the accountant.
7. **Lead source.** Tag a lead with the site it came from (Tradekey …) to see which sign-ups pay off.
8. **Backup.** A monthly spreadsheet of everything saved to our Google Drive.
9. **Sending for signature (optional, may cost).** A proper e-signature service gives the other side a link and keeps an audit trail. Free tiers exist but are limited; prices to be checked and shown before any choice – needs Chris's OK.
10. **Site alerts into the app.** Tradekey and similar sites email new buying requests; forwarded to our Gmail, Claude can read them and suggest leads (see "Checking the sites we joined" below).

## Checking the sites we joined (Chris's question)
The bot inside the app cannot log in to Tradekey or other sites: it has no browser, and we never give it passwords. Better:
- Turn on email alerts in each site, so new enquiries land in Gmail.
- Ask Claude here (this project) to sweep those emails or, when needed, to look at a site in your own browser while you are signed in – you stay in control and nothing is stored.
- Never type a site password into the app or into a chat.

## Also worth knowing (not app features)
- POPIA: a company that keeps people's details must register its information officer with the Information Regulator.
- The NCNDA, IMFPA and SPA templates still need one check by an SA commercial attorney (open since 28 Sep).
