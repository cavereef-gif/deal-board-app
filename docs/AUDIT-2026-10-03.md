Status: **report only – nothing in section C is built.** Chris, 3 Oct: "make sure all features do what they need to and that the workflow is the best it can be." Checked on the v17 prototype (branch `prototype`, after Batch 2 Parts A and B) at iPhone 8 size (375×667) and S22 size (360×780), the four locked looks plus Verve, dark and light, demo data.

How it was checked: tools/flows.py (99 everyday-job checks), tools/workflow.py (the 16 deal jobs with taps counted), tools/screens.py (268 screens per look: font sizes, contrast, coloured letters, tap targets, made-up words), tools/geometry.py (1,048 Ion Rail measurements), and looking at the screenshots. Not checked: anything that needs a real phone or the live database (push, the icon number, WhatsApp opening, the Google script, the bot's answers).

## A. What works
- 16 of 16 everyday deal jobs can be done: start a deal 4 taps · tick a step 2 · send the status 3 · make an NCNDA 3 · quote PDF 3 · chase someone 2 taps + Send · new task ≤ 5 · give a task to Both 4 · see the market price 1.
- 98 of 99 automatic checks pass (the one failure is the test's own Saturday bug, see B3). No page errors on any screen.
- Ion Rail geometry: 1,048 PASS, 0 FAIL after Parts A and B.
- Readability in Ion Rail, Twilight and Verve: no coloured letters, no heavy large text, lowest contrast 4.51 (Ion) / 5.12 (Verve), nothing under 14 px except the one old 12 px avatar initial on lead cards.
- Since today: any task's, step's and buyer-search step's words can be changed; Postpone to; names and the greeting in Settings.

## B. What is broken or clumsy (found, with evidence)
1. **The deal page is five to six phone screens tall** on the iPhone 8 (steps 6.2 screens, numbers 6.1, commission split 6.4, documents 5.2). Everything is there, but you scroll past the ticket, the facts table, the next card, the trust check and the whole procedure to reach what you came for. The 28 Sep design review's P2 ("the deal as one path") is still only proposed.
2. **Filling the 9 key terms takes 9 taps and the form is 3,200 px tall (17 rows).** On a phone that is three screens of fields at once. (workflow J2)
3. **Test bug:** "It shows under Tomorrow on Annemarie's list" fails on Fridays and Saturdays because it reads the swipe button's word instead of the group label. The app is right; the test is wrong. (docs/proposals/2026-10-03-proposed-batch-fixes.md §3)
4. **Older controls under 44 px** in the locked geometry: Accept pills 24 px (on 19 screens), Send status 24, sheet Close 28, the Chris · Annemarie · Both of us switch and the deal tabs 34. (proposal §1 – option A keeps the look and adds an invisible 44 px tap area)
5. **Graphite and Velvet contrast**: More › Documents filter count 1.07:1, People's day marks 1.23:1, the ✓ in the deal page's trust check. Ion Rail, Twilight and Verve are clean. (proposal §2)
6. **Two overlapping check lists**: the deal page's Trust check (buyer 0/5 · stockpile 0/6) and the new Buyer · Stockpile · Funds dots share two items. (docs/proposals/2026-10-03-merge-trust-and-checks.md – three decisions for Chris)
7. **Made-up words still on screen**: "brief" (34 places), "kit" (13), "route" (8), "Playbook" (4), "queue" (3), "gate" (3), "waits" (2), "Unverified", "Qualified". The plain-words rule says every one needs an everyday word or a one-line explanation.
8. **The task sheet carries eleven controls** (Done · Chased · who · due · postpone · urgent · notes · WhatsApp · ask · change the words · calendar · drop · deal) and 436 words. The three you use most (Done · Chase · Postpone) are there, but the rest is one long column.
9. **Meetings from Google Calendar cannot be moved in the app** – the app only shows them. Postponing a meeting means opening Google Calendar.
10. **Opening a buyer-search step from Today** landed on an empty People page when People or Saved was showing – **fixed today** (today.js goTo).
11. **The free database pauses after 7 idle days** (Supabase free plan). The board is used daily, so this has not bitten, but there is no keep-alive yet. (3 Oct research)
12. **Still unproven on real phones**: the icon number, the 07:00 note wording (needs the `tools` function deployed), WhatsApp opening with the text typed, the Google link script, the route map tiles.
