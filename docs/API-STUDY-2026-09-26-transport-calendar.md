## A. Transport calculator: from two place names to distance, time, tolls and diesel

| Need | Cost-saver (R0) | Best | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Truck distance and time | Geoapify truck routing – 3,000 credits a day free, business use allowed, needs a "Powered by Geoapify" line. TomTom as a check – 20,000 routes a month free, no card. | HERE truck routing – South Africa listed as full truck coverage; free amount at our volume but the exact free allowance is unconfirmed (1,000 a day without a card; 5,000–30,000 a month with one, sources disagree). | Save each route once; repeat quotes cost nothing. Flag when two services differ by more than 10%. Google: truck mode only in the USA/Japan – not suitable. GraphHopper free plan not for business. ~1–1.5 h a week. |
| Tolls (class 3 and 4) | Our own plaza table from the SANRAL 1 March 2026 poster (machine-readable PDF, covers N1, N3, N4, N17 incl. private plazas; +3.12%). The app sees which plazas the route passes. Update every 1 March. | TollGuru – South Africa on its list, heavy-class accuracy unconfirmed – US$80 (R1,303) a month. Not worth it. | Examples class 4 / class 3: Mariannhill R57/R37 · Mooi R324/R240 · Tugela R359/R260 · Wilge R304/R215 · De Hoek R230/R160 · Middelburg R365/R277 · Machadodorp R729/R510 · Nkomazi R405/R281. N3TC's site still shows 2024 prices. Moamba (Mozambique) not on the poster. HERE and Google do not confirm South African toll prices. ~30–45 min a week. |
| Diesel | Claude reads the official monthly fuel statement (Dept of Mineral and Petroleum Resources, first Wednesday) and the app shows the new price as "Suggested" – one tap. Cents a month. | Fuel SA feed – R59 a month (history to 2008, coastal and inland). | Sept 2026 wholesale 50ppm: R30.05 inland, R28.79 coastal [third-party]. October outlook about +R3.01/L [third-party]. Litres = distance × a set "litres per 100 km" per truck type. |
| Place names → map pins | Geoapify (same free credits); Nominatim as back-up (1 a second, no search-as-you-type, must save results). | Google place search – 5,000 free a month then US$32 (R521) per 1,000; pins may only be kept 30 days. | Rural farms and sidings are often missing from free maps: pin each farm, mine or siding once in the Directory and reuse it. ~20–30 min a week. |
| Public holidays | Nager.Date – free, South Africa included (4 Nov 2026 election day already added). | Same. | Due dates and "+3 days" can skip holidays. |
| Weather at mines and ports | MET Norway – free, business use allowed (must name the app, save results). | Open-Meteo commercial plan about US$29 (R472) a month. | Open-Meteo's FREE plan is for non-commercial use only – do not use it for the business. |
| Border and port queues | Claude summarises the weekly WCO/FESARTA cross-border report and the BUSA cargo update into a "Borders and ports this week" note. Cents. | Same plus one-tap links to the Border Management Authority and TRAC N4 posts. | No live feed or API exists (BMA, Transnet, TRAC). ~30 min a week. |
| Load boards | – | – | Kweli, AppLoads, Transhub: none has an API. Keep using them by hand. |
| Cost per km check | – | Road Freight Association vehicle cost schedule (members / on request, no API). | Useful once a year to check the rate per km. |

## B. Calendars and reminders

| Need | Cost-saver (R0) | Best | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| App dates into both phones' calendars | A private calendar link (.ics) from Supabase. iPhone subscribes on the phone; Google on Android refreshes only every 8–24 h and must be set up once in a computer browser. | A Google Apps Script in Chris's Gmail writes every due date and loading date into one shared "Deal Board" Google Calendar – R0. | Chris sees it in Google Calendar (and Samsung Calendar through Google sync – to confirm). Annemarie: add her Google account on the iPhone, or subscribe to the calendar's secret address. Free limits: 5,000 events a day – far more than needed. One "unverified app" warning to tap past once. ~30–45 min a week. Never put amounts, the target or limit in a calendar. |
| One tap "Add to calendar" on one item | Google Calendar link with the event filled in – works on both phones. | Same. | A calendar file (.ics) inside the iPhone home-screen app is reported to fail silently – test on the iPhone 8 first. |
| Today's meetings shown on Today | The same Apps Script reads the calendar and passes today's list to the app. | Same. | ~10 min a week. |
| Booking meetings with outside people | Free Google booking page (one page on a free Gmail, email reminders, checks all calendars) + a "Send booking link" button that opens WhatsApp. | Calendly Standard US$10 (R163) a seat a month for instant alerts; Cal.com Teams US$12 (R196). | Paid adds nothing needed now. ~15 min per meeting. |
| Reminders | Web Push (both phones; iPhone on the home screen, permission from a button) + morning email via Apps Script. | Same. | WhatsApp API reminders get dearer from 1 Oct 2026 (utility messages about R0.15 each [third-party] on top of the ~R915 a month provider fee) – hold. SMS about R0.29 each (WinSMS) – skip, Chris doesn't use SMS. Telegram bot free but a new app. |
| Port truck slots | – | – | Durban (Transnet booking systems), Richards Bay (Gate Zero "No ticket, no entry", about R5 a tonne), Maputo (new port community system, July 2026): no API for brokers. Keep slot references in the app (photo → reader) with a push reminder. |

## C. Least typing, most output – other ideas

| Idea | Cost-saver | Best | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chase and update people with one tap | wa.me link with the message already typed ("Hi Sam, checking on the VAT answer for …") – a person taps Send in WhatsApp. R0, both phones, no ban risk. | Same. | Biggest cut in typing: ~1–2 h a week. |
| Quotes, commission statements, invoices as PDF | Built on the phone (jsPDF) and sent through the share sheet to WhatsApp or email. R0, both phones. | Zoho Invoice – free in South Africa (2 users, 500 invoices a year, "Powered by" footer), API 1,000 calls a day. | ~1–2 h a week. |
| Contacts without typing | Apps Script syncs Directory contacts to one Google Contacts account → both phones' address books and WhatsApp. Contact Picker on the S22 (not iPhone). | Same. | ~30–60 min a week. iPhone .vcf needs "Create New Contact" – "Done" does not save. |
| Documents filed per deal | Apps Script saves attachments and uploads into a Drive folder per deal (15 GB free); the app keeps only the link. | Same. | Supabase Storage free is only 1 GB. ~1 h a week. |
| Claude costs down, output up | Prompt caching (cached instructions cost 10% of normal), Batch 50% off for non-urgent photo/PDF reads, structured answers – no extra fee. | Web search on Sonnet 5 for "look up this company / port notice" with a source link – US$10 per 1,000 searches (about R0.16 each), capped; about R50–150 a month for 100 lookups (estimate). | Needs Chris's OK for web search. Haiku 4.5 $1/$5, Sonnet 5 $2/$10 per million tokens. |
| E-signing NCNDA / IMFPA / ICPO | DocuSeal free cloud – 10 signature requests a month. | Zoho Sign API – US$0.50 (R8) per envelope, no monthly fee. | South Africa (ECTA s13): an ordinary e-signature is valid when the parties accept it – add "the parties agree to sign electronically" to the documents. Not legal advice; foreign buyers' laws may differ. ~1 h a week. |
| Ship tracking | One-tap link to the ship's public page by IMO number. | VesselFinder credits – smallest purchase €330 (R6,131) upfront; Datalastic €199 (R3,697) a month. | Paid feed not approved – not recommended. |
| Glue services (n8n, Make, Zapier) | Not needed – Supabase + Apps Script cover it. | – | – |
