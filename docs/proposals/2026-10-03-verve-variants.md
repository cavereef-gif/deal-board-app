# Verve look – three colour proposals (3 Oct 2026, evening)

Status: **proposed – not approved, not built.** Chris asked for more proposals with the Verve colours: more khaki, off-white on a lighter white, every colour in use, orange used carefully.

All three are real renders of the app (iPhone 8 size, night and day, Today and a deal) built only from the website's recorded design system ("Nightshift→Steel", HANDOVER-verve-website-18sep2026.md on Drive) and the logo file: ground #0B0E10 · pitch #12161A · steels #1C2124 / #4C555B / #8C979E · slab #878B91 · cream #F4F4F2 · bone #E6E9EA · khaki (the VA monogram) #B8B2A8 / #A29C94 · the logo's warm near-black #1B1916 / #26231F · burnt orange #D2621E as the live dot only.

- **A · Steel** – what is on the prototype now (2026-10-03-verve-A-steel.png).
- **B · Khaki** – the logo's warm near-black frame; khaki chrome on main buttons, the now line, nodes and the selected tab; cream cards with bone chips; day = a khaki frame on a bone page (2026-10-03-verve-B-khaki.png). Measured: nothing under 5.3:1 at night, 5.5:1 by day. **Recommended.**
