# Deal Board – automation API study (25 Sep 2026)

Status: STUDY ONLY – NOT approved for building. Nothing built, nothing signed up for. Build only after Chris answers the decisions at the end. Prices seen on 25 Sep 2026 on the providers' own pages (sources at the end). Rate used: US$1 = R16.40.

## The short answer
Most of the automation can run on what is already paid for (the Claude key in the app) plus free Google and Supabase tools. The recommended first set costs about R60 a month. The one big-ticket item is automatic WhatsApp (about R915 a month through a provider), and it needs a decision about the business number first.

Everything the automation produces arrives as "Suggested" – Chris or Annemarie still accept or drop it. The bot never saves on its own.

## Ranked by time saved

### 1. Photo of handwritten notes, business cards, slips, documents → tasks, contacts, notes
What it does: tap Camera in the app, snap the page; the app reads it and shows suggested tasks, names, numbers, dates and amounts to accept.
- Works on both phones: `<input type="file" accept="image/*" capture="environment">` opens the camera on the S22 and the iPhone 8. (The iPhone 8 can't do Apple's "Live Text" – that needs an iPhone XS or newer – so an app feature is the only way for Annemarie.) Shrink photos on the phone first (canvas, ~1,568 px long side for Haiku, up to ~2,576 px for Sonnet 5, JPEG ~0.8).
- Cost-saver: Claude Haiku 4.5 (already in the app) – about R17 a month for 100 photos + 20 PDFs. Good on print and business cards, weak on messy handwriting: in an April 2026 study of South African handwritten forms Haiku read 51% of fields right and invented text 23% of the time.
- Best (recommended): Claude Sonnet 5 ($2/$10 per million tokens) – same key, same bill – about R56 a month. Its predecessor scored 64% right / 12% invented in that study; no handwriting test of Sonnet 5 itself yet.
- Strongest on handwriting: Google Gemini (3.1 Pro 78% right / 8% invented; about R75 a month) – but it adds a second supplier and must be on the paid tier (the free tier trains on the data).
- Free but manual: Google Lens on the Samsung copies handwriting; paste it in.
- Advice: test 10 real pages (Chris's and Annemarie's handwriting) on Sonnet 5 and Gemini before choosing. Use structured outputs (JSON); mark unclear words "unclear" instead of guessing; phone numbers and amounts must be checked by a person before saving.
- Privacy: the Claude API does not train on the data and deletes it within 30 days; a data processing agreement is included (POPIA s72 allows transfer under such an agreement – mention it in the privacy notice).

### 2. Email into the app
What it does: put a Gmail label (e.g. "Deal Board") on an email; within 5 minutes it lands in the app as a note or suggested task on the right deal or person.
- Cost-saver and best: a small Google Apps Script on the Gmail account (label → time trigger every 5 min → POST to a Supabase Edge Function → remove label) – free, about 2 hours to build, no new supplier.
- Alternative: Resend's free forwarding address – free up to 3,000 a month.
- Avoid: Gmail API push (restricted scopes need a Google security review; logins expire every 7 days without it).

### 3. Reminders to phones and a daily email
- Phone notifications: free (Web Push with VAPID keys from a Supabase Edge Function + pg_cron). Works on Android Chrome, and on the iPhone 8 (iOS 16.4+; the iPhone 8 gets iOS 16.7) once the app is on the home screen and permission is asked from a button tap. If "Don't allow" is tapped, the app must be removed and re-added.
- Daily email: free from the own Gmail via Apps Script (up to 100 recipients a day).
- Calendar: due dates into Google Calendar – free (Apps Script).
- WhatsApp reminders: about R0.12 each, only with a WhatsApp Business API number (see 6).

### 4. Contracts and assay reports (PDF) → deal terms and checklist
Upload an ICPO, NCNDA, SPA or assay certificate; the app suggests deal terms (grade, tonnes, price, basis, dates, parties) and which checklist steps it proves. Same Claude key: about R0.35 per 5-page PDF on Haiku, about R1.25 on Sonnet 5.

### 5. Voice notes → tasks
Hold a button, speak ("Tell the transporter the load moves Tuesday, chase the client for the VAT answer"), get suggested tasks.
- Claude can't take audio, so speech-to-text first (Edge Function), then Claude turns the text into tasks.
- Cost-saver: OpenAI gpt-4o-mini-transcribe – about R3 a month for 60 minutes. Deepgram gives US$200 free credit and lists Afrikaans.
- Best for Afrikaans: ElevenLabs Scribe – the only one publishing an Afrikaans accuracy band ("Good": 10–20% words wrong); roughly R4–R100 a month depending on plan (to confirm).
- No service publishes accuracy for the South African English accent – test 10 real mixed Afrikaans/English notes first.
- iPhone 8 can record (MediaRecorder, iOS 16), but there are reports of the microphone failing after the home-screen app is reopened – release the mic after each recording and test on the real phone. The Web Speech API does not work inside home-screen apps on iPhone.

### 6. WhatsApp chats into the app automatically
- Cost-saver (free, today): Export chat. On the Samsung the app is already a share target; on the iPhone, save to Files and pick it in the app.
- Best: move the business number to the WhatsApp Business app and connect it to the official Cloud API ("coexistence") through a provider such as 360dialog – about R915 a month (€49). Last 6 months of one-to-one chats and all new ones arrive via webhook.
  - Limits: groups not included; broadcast lists and disappearing messages stop; the phone must open WhatsApp at least every 14 days.
  - From 1 Oct 2026 Meta charges per service message (about R0.12 each in SA) for replies sent through the API; messages typed in the WhatsApp app stay free; incoming messages are free.
  - South Africa: providers disagree on whether coexistence is available here yet – confirm before paying.
- Do not use unofficial tools (whatsapp-web.js, Baileys): they break WhatsApp's terms and can get the number banned.

### 7. Counterparty checks
- CIPC official API (company, directors, beneficial ownership): R1,200 a year for 29,750 calls; 100 free a year. Sign-up reported unreliable – test first. Pay-per-check: VerifyNow R29.90 per company, R29.90 per director, R5.98 per AML/PEP check.
- Sanctions: free official lists (OFAC, UN, EU, UK, SA FIC) plus OpenSanctions at about R1.90 per query (commercial) – about R112 a month for 60 checks.
- Email/phone checks: ZeroBounce 100 free a month.

### 8. Transport maths and rates
- Truck route distance: OpenRouteService (free, HGV profile, no tolls) or HERE (free up to 30,000 routes a month, truck mode; SA toll pricing unconfirmed).
- Tolls: no SANRAL API – keep a table of the plazas on the routes, updated every 1 March.
- Diesel: type monthly, or Fuel SA feed R59 a month.
- USD/ZAR: free (Frankfurter v2 or SARB Web API).
- Chrome/manganese ore prices: no free feed; paid data (SMM about R2,185 a month; Fastmarkets/Argus by quote) not worth it now – keep a weekly price log per deal.
